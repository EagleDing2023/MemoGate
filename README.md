# MemoGate

**Flashcard before the scroll.**

MemoGate is an attention-redirection app that intercepts taps on distracting apps (Instagram, TikTok, X, etc.) and surfaces at least one flashcard before letting you through. The goal: turn dead micro-moments of doomscroll temptation into spaced-repetition study reps.

> 🌐 Live web app: [memogate.app](https://memogate.app/)
> 📱 iOS app: launching summer 2026 (Swift, via Apple Developer Program)
> 🔒 Source code is intentionally private — this repo is the public spec.

---

## The problem

I built MemoGate because I realized the absurd number of times I subconsciously open Instagram throughout the day, wasting both my time and breaking my concentration. If I were forced to do a flashcard or a quick recall of the materials I'm currently learning, I would have memorized every formula I needed through spaced repetition. This hijacks the subconscious habit of unconscious scrolling throughout the day, forcing the user to take a moment and LOCK IN — review and recall study material, and pause to think about *why* you're opening the app, breaking the auto-pilot habit loop of zombie scrolling.

Existing apps treat flashcards as a destination — you go *to* the app to study. That's the whole problem. People don't go.

Existing habit-breaking apps are too general or require heavy action. Examples:

- **Friction (the app) — Reduce Screen Time:** Stops doomscrolling by requiring you to complete a physical challenge (push-ups, squats, or a plank) to unlock your distracting apps. Not fit for everyone or public use.
- **One Sec:** Places a breathing exercise or countdown barrier between you and your distracting apps, forcing you to consciously decide if you really need to open them. Instead of breathing, we can be much more productive and have the same effect with MemoGate.

MemoGate combines Friction and One Sec. Built by a student, for students. Instead of opening brain rot, we open a flashcard and LOCK IN for our next quiz and exam.

---

## The hook

What if the flashcard came to you, in the exact moment you were about to waste time?

MemoGate sits between you and your distracting apps. When you tap Instagram, TikTok, or whatever your specific trap is, an iOS Shortcut intercepts the tap. Before the distracting app opens, MemoGate shows you at least one due card. Answer it, and then you can decide to continue or lock in. Cards can be generated by AI from any topic you name — or built manually.

Three mechanisms doing the work at once:

- **Behavioral friction.** A 5-second pause between impulse and reward weakens the habit loop.
- **The testing effect.** Active recall outperforms re-reading on long-term retention.
- **Spaced repetition.** Cards resurface on an interval tuned to forgetting curves.

The card you would have skipped becomes the card you can't skip. The time that would have been wasted becomes time used to recall — because the alternative is closing the app and giving up the reward.
